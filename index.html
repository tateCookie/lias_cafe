Our Story

Lía Cafe began with a simple idea: coffee should feel personal. Every cup is pulled to order, every toast is built fresh, and every visit is treated like the first.

"A small daily ritual, done properly."

Tucked into Muaither, Lía has become a quiet fixture of the neighborhood — a place to slow down, catch up, and taste something made with care, one cup at a time.
